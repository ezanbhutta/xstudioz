The short version

If you email us, we hold your address and whatever you wrote, because otherwise we cannot reply. If you accept cookies, two analytics tools tell us how the site is used. That is everything. Nothing is sold, nothing is used for advertising, and no profiles are built.

There is no contact form on this site, no account to create and nothing to log into. The only way to reach us is to write.

XStudioz is a brand design agency operating through Fiverr, at fiverr.com/x_studioz (opens in a new tab), and publishing this website at xstudioz.com.

What is collected

Three things, and nothing else.

  • What you send us. If you email inquire@xstudioz.com we receive your address, your name if you give it, and the contents of your message.
  • Analytics, only if you accept. Pages viewed, approximate location, device type, how you arrived, and a small number of specific actions: clicking the email address, clicking through to our Fiverr profile or our portfolio, reading a question on an FAQ page, and how far down a service page you read.
  • Server logs. Our host records requests, including IP addresses, as part of running the server. This is not something we switch on or off.

The analytics events are built so that the email address is not sent as a parameter. A click on the email link is recorded as a click and the link text, and nothing more.

No special category data is collected. Nothing is knowingly collected from children. There is no automated decision-making and no profiling.

How long it is kept

Some of this is fixed by the platforms involved and is simply a fact. The rest is ours to decide and has not been decided yet.

Set by Microsoft, not by us

Clarity's retention is Microsoft's, and we can neither extend nor shorten it. Session recordings are kept for 30 days. Click and aggregated page data, heatmaps, and any session we label or favourite are kept for 9 months. Microsoft deletes the data from its servers and backups afterwards. The cookie policy sets this out in a table.

Still to be decided

  • Correspondence and files for projects that do go ahead, including any period tax or accounting records require.
  • Google Analytics data, which is a retention setting inside the GA4 property. [CONFIGURATION REQUIRED]
  • Server logs, which the host retains on its own schedule.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask for a copy of what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, ask for processing to be restricted, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and ask for your data in a portable form. Where consent is the basis you can withdraw it at any time, and withdrawing does not affect anything that was lawful beforehand.

Write to inquire@xstudioz.com. A response is due within one month. There is no charge.

Complaining

If you think your data has been handled badly, tell us and we will try to put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, and you do not need our agreement to do so. That right exists whether or not we are on the ICO register.

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
